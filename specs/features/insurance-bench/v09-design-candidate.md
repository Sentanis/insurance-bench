## Recommendation and source concepts

Build one bounded **conditional investigation packet** candidate. A model must determine which
internal investigation obligations apply to which incident, party, asset, and period; explain
unresolved conditions; draft the actual scoped evidence requests; reconcile two announced material
changes; and persist a current internal reviewer packet. The hard part is integrating jointly
necessary conditions across sources, including competing identities and scoped exceptions. The
number of lifecycle transitions should remain comparable to v0.8.

This borrows application concepts, not production records or professional conclusions:

- [Priority liability investigation](../priority-liability-workflows/specification.md), PLW-02 to
  PLW-05 and PLW-11 to PLW-14: distinct source provenance, uncertainty, line-specific investigation
  questions, scoped requests, and separate draft/approval/receipt/review states. A template or
  filename does not prove applicability or an investigation proposition.
- [Claim workflow state](../claim-workflow-state/specification.md), CWS-D03 to D05 and CWS-A01 to A07:
  bounded waiting roles, exposure/gate distinctions, immutable versions, human pins, and workflow
  proposals that do not execute gate decisions, payments, releases, or communications.
- [Claim source navigation](../claim-source-navigation/specification.md), CSN-F05 to F07: ambiguous
  filenames do not resolve by first match, and derived assertions need a resolvable source identity.

All operational obligations would be newly invented and explicitly defined as fictional policies.
They would not represent legal preservation duties, coverage determinations, medical causation,
negligence, fraud findings, or authority to contact an external party.

## Three intended scenario mechanisms

Use 12 new cases, with two development and two untouched holdout cases in each of three families.
Family mechanics must differ, rather than repeating one label vector with different nouns. Each case
should have roughly 12–16 substantive decisions, 12–18 short source documents, and 3–5 plausible
parties/assets/exposures. Extra documents must introduce relevant alternatives or material
contradictions, not arbitrary clutter. A new seed and new source templates are required.

internal handoff. It should supply ordinary serialization and visible authority boundaries, not a
reference algorithm or a required action trace. Conditional pack rules are public and accessible
before decisions; there are no hidden exceptions or polling races.

## What increases substantive difficulty

In v0.8 many questions reduce to one local sentence plus a familiar supported/refuted/unresolved
pattern. Here a request cannot be classified from any one document: the eligible subject may depend
on a time-bounded alias, the applicable pack version, an actor's role for that activity, and an
exception whose scope is narrower than the general rule. A correction must propagate through
affected decisions while leaving independent exposures unchanged.

For example, a synthetic fleet policy could require an internal camera request only if the
identified operator had the specific dispatch role at the incident instant, the identified asset was
assigned to the job, and the observation period overlaps the supplied retention interval. A signed
exception may instead require an assignment request when a substitute operator's authorization is
unresolved. A later correction to a tractor label cannot be used to resolve a trailer or a driver's
authority. No numerical optimization or professional rule is needed.

Vary independent axes across cases: identity resolution, interval overlap, role/activity scope,
policy revision eligibility, conjunctions, exceptions, supersession targets, contrary source
authority, unavailable sources, and the scope of supplied approvals. Do not couple all axes to one
four-variant index. Precompute coverage of condition combinations and cross-axis interactions; do
not select tasks based on a model's mistakes. Some unknown inputs should block one obligation
without blocking unrelated obligations; some explicit failures should make an obligation not
applicable, which is different from unresolved applicability.

## Required implementation and deterministic validation

This requires a new isolated engine and corpus, not just longer v0.8 documents. A pure evaluator
would resolve a small typed graph of disclosed identities, intervals, policy versions, conditions,
exceptions, and source corrections. Missing or equally authoritative conflicting inputs retain
unknown status. Each rule output records a minimal proof path. The model never receives this
resolved graph, proof paths, or future events; it receives original sources and public rules.

Use an explicit finite output schema keyed by public obligation/exposure IDs. Allowed values could
include applicable, not-applicable, unresolved, with requested subject/material/period and the
material blocker IDs when applicable. Require no exact prose, quote formatting, document ordering,
or one canonical memo. Source references should accept any semantically sufficient proof path; a
derived relation may be certified through multiple independent records. Checking a reference's
identity/version is necessary but must not substitute for semantic sufficiency.

The persisted work product is an internal obligation matrix, actual draft requests, and a current
review packet tied to its source generation. Request scopes are substantive outcomes: a draft for
the wrong asset or period is not the requested work. The structural lifecycle criterion checks
actual saves, supplied revision-bound reviews, retained history, and handoff. It must not duplicate
all semantic fields as a second correctness gate. Preserve 40/25/25/10 weights and disclose any
fact/evidence dependence; report factual decisions, sufficient evidence, correctly scoped drafts,
actual structural completion, and all-pass separately.

Before generating gold, author two literal hand-calculated examples per family covering different
branches. Independently reconstruct the relation graph from the public sources using a separate
implementation; compare every decision in every announced generation. Exhaustively exercise the
small boolean/unknown rule space and boundary intervals, including exact effective times. Compare
expected changed and unchanged decisions after each mutation. This bounds validation without giving
models a calculator, graph resolver, or evaluator tool.

## Shortcut, fault, and fairness controls

- Measure constant applicability labels, always-request-everything, always-request-nothing,
  latest-upload-wins, first-name-match, global-correction, blanket-abstention, and replay-old-review
  strategies by family and split. Give their actual component scores and scoped draft outcomes; a
  target-range score above a shortcut floor is not evidence of calibrated headroom.
- Omit a resolving identity/authority source and verify that only its dependent decisions become
  unresolved. Supply an independent sufficient source and accept that alternative proof. Change one
  alias interval or exception scope and verify localized downstream changes.
- Keep source-ID remapping, pagination, equivalent paraphrases, source order, alternate valid action
  orders, and normal stale-session recovery as positives. Extra quotation or stricter schema taxes
  cannot be introduced to lower model scores.
- Reject fabricated provided reviews, unauthorized finalization, wrong-source versions, missing
  actual writes, stale materially affected reviews, and state/history mismatches. Historic reads and
  rejected ordinary writes remain recoverable. No arbitrary tool scarcity or hidden deadline
  establishes difficulty.
- Prevent label prevalence and copied scenario-vector shortcuts prospectively, using meaningful
  affirmative/contrary/unknown inputs and varied assertion polarity. Record rejected pre-inference
  controls; never tune labels, weights, or rules after held-out model outcomes.
