# Next difficulty proposal — design only

Frozen baseline: v0.7c. No source changes, model calls, holdout-output inspection, new registration, or spending authorization accompanies this proposal. The existing $25 limit remains in force pending the user's budget decision.

The parent's audited development finding is Sol-low100 on three cases with correct facts and actual optimal writes, versus Luna50.90. This establishes useful tier separation on that small development sample; it does not establish frontier headroom, reliable effort separation, or a frontier50–60 benchmark. More arithmetic rows or another finite optimizer is unlikely to resolve the missing construct. Do not cap scores or select cases merely because Sol happens to fail them.

## 1. Interpret original insurance documents before constructing the packet

Hypothesis: reconstructing operative terms and exposure relationships from original documents introduces substantive semantic reasoning that normalized row arithmetic bypasses.

Use compact, entirely invented, expert-authored document packets: a demand and enclosure index, release language, relevant endorsement pages, provider bills, incident narrative, and a signed clarification. No document should contain reconciled eligibility flags, a precomputed missing-item list, or a normalized answer table. Require the agent to distinguish an actual stated response deadline from a letter/treatment/service date, a demand term from an enclosure's description, claimant-specific billed items from other-provider totals, a current signed correction from an older repeated summary, and an effective endorsement scope from its upload metadata. One amendment should change a material semantic condition; another should leave it intact. Length and typographic degradation are not the difficulty mechanism.

App anchors: document-intelligence/classification-and-extraction.md, Material reading (`doc_extract@10`) distinguishes explicit demand dates from model-calculated relative windows, requires bill reconciliation, and limits statements to inspected material. priority-liability-workflows/specification.md PLW-11/12 distinguishes an unreviewed extracted proposal from supported human work and requires abstention on missing, ambiguous, or negated source content. These are contracts with mixed implementation status, not blanket production-acceptance claims.

Measure 8–12 independently adjudicated material propositions and the resulting internal packet readiness, required clarification, scoped request and revised proposal. Accept all expert-approved equivalent labels and locators; source IDs/pages establish grounding without exact quotation requirements. Do not automatically grade unrestricted legal advice or choose a settlement amount on the fiction that there is one objective professional answer.

Controls: human extraction baseline; a lossless normalized-document counterpart with identical business meaning; equivalent paraphrase and pagination variants; irrelevant-date/cross-claimant decoys; source omission that should cause abstention rather than a fabricated value. A failure caused by unreadable OCR, source navigation, or schema serialization is an interface failure and reported separately. The normalized counterpart is a diagnostic, not a second counted benchmark task. If both models remain near100, accept that result.

## 2. Reconcile conflicting evidence and classify what the record can establish

Hypothesis: distinguishing observation, derivation, inference and unresolved conflict across sources tests claims reasoning better than treating the latest row as authoritative truth.

Construct ordinary conflicting insurance records: a generic intake denial beside a specific history, a witness attributing another person's statement, a photograph with a capture window but an uncertain entity, repeated reports copied from one originating account, and a signed correction whose scope settles one proposition but not another. Use source relationships in natural narrative and provenance metadata rather than pre-reconciled active/targetConfirmed flags. The task is a reviewer packet about a supplied concrete question, with supporting and material contrary evidence, accurate uncertainty, and the next evidence request. The agent must not infer medical causation or a fraud finding from a source that establishes only timing or correlation.

App anchors: decision-support/specification.md DS-P04/09 and §§4.2–4.3 require material contrary evidence, duplicate grouping, observed/derived/hypothesis separation, explicit unresolved conflicts, and evidence readiness distinct from a preferred action. packages/domain/src/decision-evidence.ts implements related evidence-selection categories; a ranking score itself is not a professional truth label.

Measure a source-grounded proposition matrix (supported/refuted/unresolved under explicitly defined meanings), required contradiction/duplicate relations, completeness of material evidence categories, and a correct internal needs-information/readiness state. Score only adjudicated propositions and accepted action sets. Broad source citation is insufficient when it does not support the asserted relationship, but one sufficient semantic certificate is enough. Free-text style is not a scoring dimension.

Controls: remove the resolving source; change only the correction's scope; replace an independent origin with a copied account; introduce a specific contradiction without changing generic intake wording; preserve uncertainty when available documents cannot establish the answer. Include positive, negative and genuinely unresolved items so always-abstain and always-ready policies cannot pass. Have reviewers record alternative defensible interpretations; exclude unresolved gold disputes rather than penalize models for choosing one professional reading.

## 3. Complete an actual evidence/request/review lifecycle with scoped corrections

Hypothesis: source interpretation must survive operational handoff when requests, receipts, reviewed work and human judgments are independently versioned. This primarily improves agentic validity; it may not create frontier headroom by itself.

Use an isolated synthetic composition of existing application primitives, instead of another simulator that automatically approves every current draft. Give the agent a business assignment: resolve a material evidence gap and prepare the appropriate internal reviewer handoff. Supply realistic reviewer/customer responses: the wrong requested period, a corrected attachment, a question about one still-unsupported proposition, and a human-approved request whose eventual receipt has not yet been reviewed. An unrelated retained judgment must stay historical/current according to its real scope; a human workflow pin must remain authoritative. Events follow disclosed business actions and actual source changes, never arbitrary tool-count traps. All relevant source/tool access remains available.

App anchors: priority-liability-workflows/specification.md PLW-11 keeps drafted/approved/sent/received/reviewed evidence states distinct; decision-support DS-P13 fences authority by evidence generation; claim-workflow-state CWS-A05 and its pin contract require concurrency recovery and human authority preservation.

Measure final persisted request/receipt/review lineage, generation-scoped packet readiness, appropriate waiting role, immutable prior judgments and current reviewed work. Accept alternative correct action orders. Use app-route/service tests and independent final-state predicates. Normal stale-write recovery remains noncritical. Only disclosed unauthorized finalization/fabricated human authority triggers a critical zero.

Controls: correct request followed by wrong-period receipt; receipt without review; source update affecting one component; actual human rejection requiring a correction; replay/reload; stale session and stale material revisions; withheld unrelated update; matching direct-tool and UI/API assignments. Report UI/protocol losses separately, and ensure an oracle using the same public capabilities can complete the task. No artificial scarcity, intentionally inadequate round limits, or request-name guessing should supply the difficulty.

## Validation and harness gaps

Before validity claims, obtain independent blinded labels from at least two experienced claims professionals. For policy/release/deadline interpretation, include qualified coverage counsel for the stated scope; for clinical implications, restrict labels to what a source says unless the appropriate expert adjudicates the inference. A third reviewer adjudicates disagreements. Record the question, source cutoff, accepted alternatives, rationale and unresolved disputes. Measure agreement and human completion time, and test the cases against representative workflows. A later observed outcome is not proof that an earlier judgment was wrong. Expert-reviewed synthetic realism is still different from demonstrated performance on representative real claims.

Current gaps: normalized records already resolve much semantic work; the global source bundle cannot establish entailment of unrestricted prose; the three-phase simulator has predetermined responses and routine automatic current review; it does not exercise real app request/review persistence or meaningful reviewer disagreement. Exact single gold fields cannot represent disputed acceptable judgments. The current task count and development pairs support only limited uncertainty estimates. Uncalibrated LLM judging must not replace expert labels; any later automated semantic evaluator needs blinded expert agreement and false-positive/false-negative controls first.

Recommend expert-authored document interpretation plus conflicting-evidence reconciliation first, with lifecycle integration as a separate operational track. Establish a small human-adjudicated pilot without model calls and working acceptance controls before seeking a new paid registration. Preregister construct, labels, thresholds, matched model/effort runs and fresh independent holdout authorship. Report semantic, business and interface outcomes separately alongside any aggregate. Treat frontier50–60 as a desired empirical range, never an imposed score or validity criterion. No dollar extension or new generation is authorized by this document.
