Insurance Bench measures how well model-and-harness combinations complete insurance work: find
relevant evidence, resolve conflicting documents, calculate exposures and clocks, prepare a
reviewable brief, and propose the next operational step. It is a separate research tool beside
Sentanis, informed by its source-level contracts; its score does not authorize claim decisions or
changes to the deployed application.

The first edition is a closed-universe synthetic pilot covering six families: deadline triage,
coverage review, financial reconciliation, evidence briefs, fraud referrals, and workflow handoffs.
The pilot contains 24 cases, two development and two holdout cases in each family. All records and
scenario rules are invented. No production, staging, local-live or Helix records are read, sampled,
fitted, anonymized or copied. Similarity means shared concepts and workflow shape, not measured
representativeness of the production book.
