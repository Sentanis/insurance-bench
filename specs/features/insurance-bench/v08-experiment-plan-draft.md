## Hypotheses and measurements

- **H1 — narrative evidence:** the same observations expressed in original narrative sources
  cause more substantive label/certificate errors than a normalized observation representation.
  A paired Sol-high development diagnostic holds rules, propositions, sources/provenance,
  business events and actions constant. Normalization must never provide verdicts or gold.
  If labels/outcomes remain at the ceiling, this hypothesis fails to establish frontier headroom.
- **H2 — attribution and scope:** models distinguish direct observation from copied reports,
  identity/period/role scope, partial corrections and conflicting accounts. Independently audit
  each terminal wrong label and semantic certificate against public fictional rules; report the
  error categories and uncertainty. Source-ID/paraphrase/page controls must pass before inference.
  This is an error-analysis hypothesis, not a causal treatment estimate.
- **H3 — work completion:** reasoning effort improves current scoped requests, supplied receipt
  review and actual revision-bound handoff beyond factual accuracy alone. Matched within-model
  low/high panels keep source, tasks, tool contract and output ceiling fixed. Report factual,
  evidence, structural workflow and all-pass differences separately; retain flat or negative gains.

## Proposed fixed order, subject to prospective affordability

1. Sol-high original, Sol-low original, Luna-low original, Luna-high original on both development
   cases in each of three correlated families, once per case. This is four bounded conditions,
   not a full provider slate.
2. Astra-high original on the same six development cases, once each, measures the available
   flagship before final holdouts. Sol-high normalized-observation diagnostic on the exact corresponding six development cases,
   once each. Analyze paired correspondence IDs explicitly; the changed dataset is not an exact
   `compareRuns` match and is not an additional six independent tasks.
3. If the reviewed generation is valid, freeze untouched holdouts before reading their outputs.
   Run Sol-high and Astra-high on both holdouts per family, once each, in that order. Astra is the
   available OpenAI flagship reference; Sol is the workhorse control. No held-out output may
   change its own source, gold, weights, selection or protocol.
4. Additional provider or prompt treatments require a separately committed prospective
   registration and affordability check. No outcome-selected task/model reruns.
