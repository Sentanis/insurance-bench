## Diversified stateful portfolios

Target **24 new synthetic cases across six operational families**, 12 development and **12 fresh
holdout**, with two cases per split/family. Fresh holdout remains untouched until final freeze.
All records, rules, people, dates, costs and priorities are invented; no live claims or distribution
fitting enters case design.

The corpus uses **three reviewers**, denser coupled resource/dependency problems and a planned
mandatory dependency closure of approximately **20–23 of 24 jobs**, with distinct topologies across
families and cases. Exact graph parameters, appointments and feasibility are recorded in the
frozen corpus, rather than assumed from these design targets. Density or document length alone is
not proof of difficulty. Vary meaningful dependency joins, reviewer qualification/availability,
release/deadline windows, approval-version conditions, budget and optional-job tradeoffs; independently
verify that mandatory work is actually achievable.

Agents must infer current eligible jobs from raw approvals/conditions, construct a feasible schedule,
reserve it through simulated tools, inspect actual state and revise conflicts. Family contexts
remain insurance review operations; common scheduling tools do not by themselves establish six
independent insurance-domain capabilities. Distinct graph topologies improve diversity but do not
remove all shared-template correlation or supply insurance-expert realism.

Public rules retain:

- reviewer qualifications, contiguous availability, appointment/release/deadline constraints;
- predecessor completion and current scoped/version-matched signed approvals;
- one booking per job, no overlapping reviewer work and the shared credit budget;
- **critical mandatory reviews**, explicitly identified before outcomes: missing or late required
  work is a critical failure; no post hoc critical labels are introduced;
- maximizing the supplied priority-unit sum among feasible mandatory-complete schedules, with
  **all equally optimal plans accepted** and no canonical schedule string.

Priority units remain synthetic operator policy, not real expected loss/ROI. Rejected exploratory
bookings can be corrected; classify the final schedule and genuine forbidden action attempts
separately. Narrating a plan cannot substitute for reserved state.

## Fixed score, evidence and validity controls

Keep **facts 40%, evidence 25%, workflow 25%, boundaries 10%**, weighted partial scores, strict
all-pass and critical rates. A frontier **50–60 weighted score** remains an honest aspirational
difficulty target. Do not force it with caps, rescaling, reweighting, stricter phrasing or unrelated
citations. Preserve raw components alongside critical-zero task scores so a lower aggregate can be
traced to substantive planning obligations rather than quote/protocol failures.

Gold controls must establish feasible mandatory-complete solutions, current approval truth,
correct closure/cost/time calculations and the exact optimal priority objective. An independently
implemented constraint evaluator checks solver plans; small controls are checked by another solver,
exhaustive enumeration or manual solutions. Require alternative optimal plans to pass where they
exist. Mutation controls reject scope/version mistakes, missing dependencies, overlap, infeasible
windows, duplicate jobs, budget overflow and missed mandatory reviews. Never expose hidden gold
solutions/optimum through tools or prompt serialization.

Evidence uses limited, minimally sufficient fact-specific proofs and predefined valid alternatives.
An existential claim such as “this reviewer qualifies for critical work” needs the staff credential
and an adequate qualifying job witness, not every qualifying job. Aggregate/closure claims still
need their indispensable operands. An optimum certificate requires an actual verified optimal
reserved schedule. Link source rows/identifiers to their relevant fields; do not accept unrelated
same-document field fragments merely because their literal strings occur somewhere. Conversely,
do not reject an equivalent sufficient source simply because it omits irrelevant boilerplate.

Before paid runs, run semantic alternate-proof and wrong-proof controls, inspect source/derived
proof sufficiency and retain audit evidence. These are engineering validity checks, not independent
insurance-expert calibration.

All conditions use **20 turns, 16384 inclusive output tokens per call and a 300-second request
deadline**. Record finish reasons, visible/hidden reasoning usage where available, successful-call
cost, reserved spend, turns and elapsed time. Any truncation invalidates a clean paired capability
contrast. Retain raw outputs and preregister common-limit reruns of every affected condition rather
than selectively improving one model's allowance.

- **FC-1, substantive discrimination:** new feasible, coupled planning tasks reduce factual/planning
  saturation through actual schedule decisions rather than extra source plumbing.
- **FC-2, effort:** high effort improves exact-ID performance across several paired cases; retain
  null/negative and critical-failure outcomes.
- **FC-3, tier:** configured Sol/Luna and Qwen tiers show reproducible case-level differences, without
  assuming parameter count alone or guaranteed monotonicity.
- **FC-4, measurement:** lower weighted outcomes correspond to semantic constraints, authority or
  actual state/optimization failures, distinguished from format, proof and protocol artifacts.

Average repeats within each case, then report paired score/component/all-pass deltas, wins/ties/losses
and critical patterns. Preserve the **10,000-resample six-family-cluster bootstrap, seed 1729**.
With six clusters and related tool mechanics, intervals are descriptive and have limited power;
claim neither a stable industry rank nor population safety accuracy. Preselect profiles/cases before
scores; name incomplete provider coverage and exclude it from clean full-panel comparisons.
