How to read these results

Scores average families equally. Evidence v0.8, investigation v0.9, interaction v0.10, allocation interaction v0.11 and dependency allocation v0.12 headline completed tasks whose existing criteria all pass; weighted partial credit is shown alongside for diagnosis. Three-case once-per-family panels resolve task success in33.33-point steps, so they cannot establish a continuous50–60 difficulty range. Earlier generations retain their original weighted score. Within each diagnostic task: facts 40%, evidence 25%, workflow 25%, boundaries 10%. Critical failures zero the task. All-pass requires every criterion. Actual outcome reports the recorded current business state for dynamic workflows; coupled v0.7 additionally requires an actually recorded feasible objective-optimal proposal subset. Static portfolios report feasible mandatory-complete optimal reservations for static portfolios, independently of citation and memo credit. Missing provider access is recorded separately; incomplete provider coverage has no headline score. Compare rows only when their task split, dataset and harness versions match.

This small pilot has no insurance expert validation. Exact structured facts and evidence are checked deterministically; prose quality and real-world legal correctness are not established. Development experiments inform harness changes; holdout results remain separate. One run cannot establish a stable model ranking. The historical v0.2 runs have a 4096-token output ceiling. Challenge v0.3, portfolio v0.4 and frontier v0.5 runs use a 16384-token output ceiling, including hidden reasoning where applicable. Portfolio agents must actually reserve prerequisite reviews under approved staff, calendar, deadline and budget constraints; missing mandatory critical reviews zero the task. Every equally optimal feasible plan is accepted. The frontier candidate uses three reviewers with varied prerequisite graphs and a common 300-second provider deadline. The six static portfolio labels share scheduling mechanics and do not establish independent domain breadth. Workflow v0.6 instead uses distinct domain derivations, scripted customer responses, two announced material updates, current revision-bound review and recorded business mutations. Coupled v0.7 reuses the reviewed revision protocol with raw occurrence/exposure/financial/demand records, shared and claimant capacities, and up to 12 competing proposal packages. Selecting the optimal proposal is only a small fraction of the weighted score (a wrong selected plan alone loses about3.57points); report actual optimal written outcomes separately and do not attribute headline differences chiefly to optimization. Its exact optimizer is grader-only; models receive no calculator or solver tool. All optimal ties are accepted, and an asserted optimum without a matching actual state write receives no current-outcome credit. The three coupled task labels share a correlated occurrence engine and do not establish independent domain breadth.
